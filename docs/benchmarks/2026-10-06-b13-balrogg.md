# 2026-10-06 → 10-15: B13, balrogg packs Ogg audio — 1.112% → 13.181% on music

**Machine:** Dell Inspiron 15-3567, i5-7200U (2 cores / 4 threads), 15.9 GB
(`Win32_Processor` checked). **Every timing below was taken on a contended
machine** — a WSL VM using ~2.7 of 4 logical cores on 2026-10-06, and another
development session on 2026-10-15, with the app run at below-normal
priority. Sizes, ratios and restore results don't depend on that; times do,
by up to 3.5× (the same three files took 85 s and then 290 s at `-9` an hour
apart). **No time here is a pin.**

Tool: balrogg release **1.4** (`github.com/iczelia/balrogg`, 2026-09-10),
`balrogg-x86_64.exe`, 247,315 B, SHA-256
`b3a52670bf37eaf941933bf67fbfdcd140638240fa3d20bb9e9bd6c923097db2` — GitHub's
published asset digest, matched on download twice and now pinned in
`toolfetch.TOOL_MANIFEST`. GPL-3.0, driven as a subprocess. **The binary's
own banner says "balrogg 1.3";** the hash is what identifies it.

The handoff's game-audio corpus (FMOD's *Celeste* project) never arrived, and

## 3. Through the app (`engine.compress`, D9 included), then `engine.extract` and an independent SHA-256 of every file

| corpus | profile | before (B13a) | **with B13** | packed | verified | compress (incl. D9) | restore |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| music | Normal (`-4`) | 1.112% | **13.181%** (147,571,088 B) | 18/18 | 18/18, 0 mismatches | 1,039 s (pack 672 s, D9 349 s) | 506 s |
| music2 | Normal (`-4`) | 0.297% (B13a's piping, measured 2026-10-15) | **10.785%** (171,387,133 B) | 24/26 (2 refused, stored) | 26/26, 0 mismatches | 983 s (pack 713 s, D9 250 s) | 222 s |

**The first app run of this branch saved 0.2%,** with the ledger showing
**1 file of 18** packed. Nothing was wrong with the codec. `oggpack` killed
any decode whose output file grew past the original's size, and balrogg
writes through a file mapping that grows **1 MiB ahead of the data** and
truncates at exit (a 14,611 B decode showed 1,048,576 B mid-run). So the
round-trip check in `pack()` failed at random and the file was stored raw:
the safe failure, which made it a silent one. The adversarial review found
it, reproduced it, and the watch now allows 2 MiB with the exact size
re-checked after exit. **A green suite missed it** because the fixtures
decode in 0.15 s, before the first 0.25 s poll.

**The restore is slow, and it is now most of a job's tail.** Decoding 147 MB
of packed Ogg took 349 s in D9 and 506 s in extract on music, and music2's
171 MB took 250 s and 222 s: about 0.3–0.8 MB/s,
against ~1.5 MB/s per file from the CLI. The difference is unexplained: low
priority beside another session explains some of it, but how much was not
measured. The estimator's `_PACK_DECODE_RATE` (1.5 MB/s) is
therefore **optimistic by up to ~3×** until a quiet-machine run settles it.
It is recorded here, not re-pinned, because a contended number may not be
pinned.

## 4. What the review found (2026-10-06, 3 lenses + a skeptic per finding)

37 findings. 27 reached a skeptic: **21 confirmed (17 distinct defects), 6
refuted**. The other 10 lost their skeptic to an API outage and were
re-checked by hand on 2026-10-15: **5 reproduced** (an Ogg folder still told
"~0%", steered to Fast and listed under "didn't shrink"; the manifest
budget 1 byte short per codec key, with stale comments beside it; the first
"pack" report throttled away; no test that the table and the bar agree, so a
pack-time double count survived; an in-sample test described as
out-of-sample), 3 were
already fixed, and 2 are not defects of this branch (no balrogg on CI is
covered by the local suite; an E1 discovery test has always skipped).
**22 distinct defects fixed in all.** Beyond the decode watch above: Cancel never reached balrogg during a restore (a
120-second test decode ran to the end); every packed member was held in RAM
until the zip was written (~10 GB for a 12 GB Ogg library); scratch files
went to the system temp dir, not the job's; the planner re-hashed the binary
once per Ogg file; the estimate predicted `-9`'s saving for every profile and
booked 0.97 instead of 1.0 as the disk-preflight worst case; J7 calibration
learned the pack phase as codec time; the `pack` progress stage was appended
as an unplanned chain stage, which could move the bar backwards; Measure
presets shared one number between profiles that pack at different efforts;
balrogg inherited `BLR_*` environment variables. Each has a test that fails
without its fix (mutation-checked).

## 5. Not done, on purpose

- `.wem` (Wwise) and `.fsb` (FMOD) are **not** handled: balrogg reads only an
  Ogg container. They need an unwrapping step first, and no corpus exists.
- Ogg FLAC and Skeleton-wrapped Vorbis are stored as they are.
- The decode rate and per-file overhead are priors from contended runs (§3).
